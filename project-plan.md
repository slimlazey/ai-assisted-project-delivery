# Project Plan — CivicPay Implementation

Delivery was broken into five phases.

## 1. Requirements
Clarify ambiguity in client requests, document as formal requirements, and
identify cross-feature dependencies before anything reaches Jira.

## 2. Development Coordination
Track Jira progress against the plan, monitor blockers raised by the dev
team, and coordinate between development and the client on open questions.

## 3. QA / UAT
Track test case execution, log and prioritize defects, and unblock UAT
participants (municipal staff) when they hit issues during testing.

## 4. Change Management
Analyze incoming change requests for downstream impact before committing to
a timeline or scope change.

## 5. Release Readiness
Review open defects, confirm UAT sign-off status, and formally assess
go-live readiness with the client.

## Timeline (illustrative)

| Phase | Approx. duration |
|---|---|
| Requirements | Weeks 1–2 |
| Development Coordination | Weeks 2–8 |
| QA / UAT | Weeks 7–10 |
| Change Management | Ongoing, weeks 4–11 |
| Release Readiness | Weeks 11–12 |
